ATTUNE: HOW TO RUN IT
Version {VERSION}, a first build for testers.

Attune is a set of small tech demos of game mechanics designed from the start for
players with physical disabilities. Every demo reads one input layer, tuned to your own
range: a single switch, a keyboard used in big zones, a mouse or trackpad, a gamepad,
your breath or a hum at the microphone.
It is for play and access. It makes no medical or therapeutic claim.


STARTING IT

1. Unzip the folder anywhere, for example onto the desktop.
2. Open the folder and run Attune.exe.
3. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC", because this test build isn't signed.
   Choose "More info", then "Run anyway".

It runs on Windows only, and it sends nothing over the internet. It doesn't use a camera
in this build. It listens to the microphone only while you play with Breath or Hum, which
work the menus too; "Microphone on" shows at the top of the screen while it listens. What it hears is measured as it arrives and never recorded or saved.

If Breath or Hum hears nothing, Windows may be blocking desktop apps from the microphone:
Settings > Privacy & security > Microphone, then turn on "Let desktop apps access your
microphone".


FIRST STEPS

- "Who's playing" opens first. Choose New profile, then the way you'll play:
  - Switch: one key or one mouse button, or a switch interface that shows up as one.
    Press it when asked, and it becomes your switch. The menus are then scanned: a ring
    moves from one choice to the next, a tap picks the ringed choice, and a long hold
    goes back.
  - Keyboard zones: any key presses. The left and right halves of the keyboard steer,
    and its four corners are four gestures, so a fist, an elbow or a mouth stick on a
    big area plays.
  - Pointer: a mouse or trackpad.
  - Gamepad.
  - Breath: breathe out at the microphone. How hard is how much, and a puff is a press.
  - Hum: hum at the microphone. Higher is more, and any hum is a press.
  With Breath or Hum the menus are scanned too: a puff or a hum picks the ringed choice,
  and a long one goes back. A mouse still works them, and F2 changes the way of playing.
- Calibration follows. It is short, and you can skip it. For Breath and Hum it first
  listens to the quiet room, then to a comfortable breath, or to your lowest and highest
  comfortable notes.
- Settings has the timing: how long a hold is, how long resting the pointer takes to
  pick something, and how fast scanning moves. Change anything that doesn't suit you.
  "Noisy room" there makes Breath ignore quieter sounds.


THE DEMOS IN THIS BUILD

- Compass Sweep: a needle sweeps round the compass. Press to sail the boat one step the
  way it points, and reach the flags. The needle waits if you don't press, so missing it
  costs nothing.
- Keyboard Garden: four beds. Each zone (or each scanned bed, with a switch) plants a
  seedling, and holding down grows it.
- Dwell Builder: build on a grid with no clicks at all. Rest the pointer on a block to
  pick it, then on a square to place it. The edge of the screen is a rest zone, where
  nothing is ever picked. With a switch, the rows are scanned instead.
- Kite Breath: breathe out to lift the kite, and it falls gently when you stop. Touch
  the ribbons as they drift past; a ribbon you miss comes round again. Holding a switch
  or a key lifts it too.
- Hum Flyer: hum higher to climb and lower to dive, and fly through the rings as they
  drift past. When you stop humming it glides down. Holding a key climbs too.

The other demos are not built yet. Their pages show what Attune reads from you.


KEYS

- Escape opens the menu in a demo. With the Switch, a long hold opens it, and with Breath
  or Hum a long breath or hum (5 seconds in the Kite Breath and the Hum Flyer).
- F2 changes to the next way of playing in the middle of a demo, with no restart.


TELLING US HOW IT WENT

The first time you open the menu in a demo, it asks "How did that feel?": Easy, Tiring,
Frustrating, Fun, or Skip. Each answer is saved as one line in a file on this computer:

    %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Jon Goldman\Attune\Feedback\

The Feedback page on the first screen shows where the file is and opens the folder.
Nothing is uploaded: please send the file back to the person who gave you this build,
with anything else you noticed.

Thank you for trying it.
